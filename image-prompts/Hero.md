# Prompt
In the style of Zhang Yimou's Hero (2002) film, a tense wuxia standoff in a dark open-air traditional Chinese chess courtyard at dusk during a heavy rainstorm. The large stone floor is itself a giant chessboard made of alternating dark and light square tiles built directly into the pavement, with no separate chessboards or scattered pieces on the ground. Jet Li as Nameless in simple dark grey and black flowing robes stands in a low, ready fighting stance, arm stretched forward, holding his straight double-edged jian sword pointed forward with focused intensity. Donnie Yen as Long Sky in lighter flowing robes stands opposite him in a strong defensive fighting stance, gripping his long silver spear ready to strike, both warriors about 20 feet apart so that sword tip and spear tips are 4 tiles away from each other. They are nearly motionless but coiled with tension, eyes locked in intense concentration, water streaming down their hair, faces, and clothes. An elderly musician sits in the background playing a guzheng. Dramatic cinematic lighting, high contrast, glistening rain droplets and puddles on the wet chessboard tiles, desaturated blue-grey-teal color palette with subtle highlights on blades and fabric, atmospheric and poetic mood, highly detailed, epic wide composition. Sword and spear directly pointed at each other in a menacing way. Both are facing each with focused attention.
