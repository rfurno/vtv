## Editorial Decisions Made

### Etymology of Kung Fu (功夫)
- **Kung (功)**: Consummation of a feat through disciplined effort; marriage of 工 (skillful work) + 力 (raw energy)
- **Fu (夫)**: Mature adult — one who has attained social standing and moral integrity through discipline. **NOT "heroic."** The character depicts a man (大) with a hairpin (簪), symbolizing coming of age.
- **Rule**: Never use "heroic" to describe 夫. It contradicts the Vedantic thesis of ego dissolution. "Person of substance" is the approved phrasing.

### Style Conventions
- **No "future piece" teasers**: End sections decisively. Don't promise sequels mid-article.
- **CTAs**: Personal and specific, not generic ("I'd love to hear..."). Frame comments as honest observation, not praise.
- **Tags**: Max 5 per article.
- **Titles**: Keep under 50 characters when possible. Subtitles can do the heavy lifting.
- **Callback over takedown**: Reference earlier articles instead of swiping at social media/TikTok. Build continuity across the newsletter.
- **Bold text**: Use sparingly. If a sentence needs bold to land, rewrite it instead.
