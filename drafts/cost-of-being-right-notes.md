# Centerline/Consent
In Mo Goon, when a Si Dai puts out his hand, he is asking for feedback. Consent is clear.
At breakfast with neighbors? No one asked for a lecture. Same with the stranger in the comment thread.
For a long time I treated every conversation like a sparring match I had to win. Better facts, clearer narrative, the quiet lift of being the one who saw more.
The old urge still shows up. What changed is I notice the cost sooner.
What conversations are you still trying to “win” that no one invited you into?
