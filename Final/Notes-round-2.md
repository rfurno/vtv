# Note 1: Don't-Ask-Me-How Rule
Sifu Leo showed me the first part of Siu Nim Tau exactly once.

# Note 3: Copycat vs Understanding
For years I thought real learning meant becoming a really good photocopy of my Sifu or Acharya.

Turns out both of them were teaching me something completely different.

Sifu didn’t want me to move like him. He wanted me to develop my own Ving Tsun.  
Acharya doesn’t want me to parrot Vedanta. He wants me to live it.

One is **model logic** (copy, repeat, look good).  
The other is **logic of self-development** (slow, messy, actually useful).

I’m still about 80% copycat on most days.
